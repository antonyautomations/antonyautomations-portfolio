2. Information We Collect

When you submit a rooftop solar enquiry form, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • City or location of the property
  • Approximate monthly electricity bill
  • Other information voluntarily provided through the form

Some contact details may be prefilled by Facebook or Instagram based on information associated with your account.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used for the following purposes:

  • To receive and register your rooftop solar enquiry.
  • To contact you regarding your solar enquiry.
  • To understand your property location and electricity usage.
  • To assess your interest in rooftop solar solutions.
  • To arrange a callback or request for a site survey.
  • To share relevant enquiry details with the solar business team.
  • To maintain enquiry records and manage follow-up communication.

4. Automated Calls and AI Assistance

To respond efficiently to solar enquiries, the solar business may use an AI-powered voice assistant to contact individuals who submit their details through the lead form.

This assistant may communicate with you about your enquiry, ask basic property and electricity usage questions, record your preferences and pass relevant information to the solar business team.

The AI assistant does not independently guarantee installation, final pricing, savings, subsidies or confirmed site-survey appointments.

You may request human assistance or ask the business not to contact you further.

5. How Information Is Processed

Information submitted through Facebook or Instagram Lead Forms may be processed using automation and communication services operated on behalf of the solar business.

Depending on the actual deployment, these services may include:

  • Meta Platforms for lead form collection.
  • n8n for workflow automation and data routing.
  • Bolna AI for AI-powered voice conversations.
  • Vobiz for telephone connectivity.
  • Google Sheets or PostgreSQL for enquiry record storage.

These services may process information as necessary to perform their respective functions. Their own privacy policies and applicable data-processing terms also apply.

6. Data Sharing

Your information may be shared with:

  • The solar business and its authorized team members.
  • Technology and communication providers supporting the service.
  • Other authorized service providers where necessary to handle your enquiry.

We do not intend to sell your personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Enquiry information may be stored in the business's designated systems for the time reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage follow-ups, maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Access to stored information should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who require it to perform their assigned functions.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be implemented to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
